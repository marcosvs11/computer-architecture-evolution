## 1.7 Problem to Solve

The Harvard architecture was motivated by the physical and throughput limitations of using a single shared pathway for both instructions and data. Early relay and vacuum-tube computers were constrained by memory access contention, where the processor could not read an instruction and read or write data at the same time
